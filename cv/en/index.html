Pneumonia detection on X-rays

Help screen for pneumonia on chest X-rays, where a missed case costs far more than a false alarm. As a team we compared seven approaches, from classical models (SVM, random forest, XGBoost) to unsupervised learning and CNNs: a fine-tuned ResNet18 reaches up to 93% accuracy and 0.98 AUC on the test set. I wrote the SVM baseline, the experiment tracking shared by every model, and the medical error analysis.

Homepedia

Spot homes sold too high or too cheap for their neighborhood. Homepedia compares each sale (French DVF data) with what it should be worth from local fundamentals, maps the gap and explains it, backtested by training on 2015-2019 and evaluating on 2020-2024. Big data pipeline in PySpark (bronze, silver, gold), PostGIS, MongoDB and Streamlit. In progress, team project.
